How to play

The 52-card deck is split evenly. Each turn both players flip their top card and the higher rank (aces high) takes both. On a tie it's war: each player lays 3 cards face down and 1 face up, and the higher face-up card takes everything on the table. Ties repeat the war. A player short of cards for a war plays what they have, using their last card face up. Take all 52 cards to win.
